gymdream 私隱政策

適用於免費公開本機版 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日

公開本機版毋須開帳戶。你的年齡、體重、訓練計劃與記錄、食物與營養記錄、自訂資料都儲存在你使用的裝置上；我們不會透過這個版本收取這些記錄作雲端備份。App 不設廣告或追蹤分析。

相機、相簿與相片

只有當你選擇拍攝或揀相片時，App 才使用相機或相簿。條碼、營養標籤及餐點相片在裝置內分析，原相不會上傳或加入你的記錄；你確認後，App 只儲存你選擇的食物與營養資料。你可在 iPhone 設定更改相機／相簿權限。

匯出、刪除與外部連結

你可在「設定 → 你的資料」匯出 JSON 備份、匯入備份或刪除本機資料。匯出檔案由你選擇儲存位置；如你選擇雲端硬碟或把檔案傳給他人，之後的處理受該服務或收件人控制。備份可能含敏感的健康及飲食記錄，請妥善保管。換機或刪除 App 前請先匯出；我們無法替你找回沒有備份的本機資料。

App 內的資料來源連結會打開第三方網站；只有在你主動開啟時才會連到該網站，該網站的私隱做法不由 gymdream 控制。

聯絡我們

如你主動電郵支援，我們會收到你信中提供的內容和電郵地址，只用於處理你的查詢。請不要寄送備份檔、醫療文件或密碼。聯絡：studionorthbyte@gmail.com。

日後如啟用雲端同步或其他收集資料的功能，我們會在推出前更新本政策及 App Store 私隱資料。

gymdream Privacy Policy

For the free public local-only release · Updated 22 October 2026

No account is required for this release. Your age, weight, training plans and logs, food and nutrition logs, and custom entries stay on the device you use. We do not receive these records through this version for cloud backup. The app has no advertising or tracking analytics.

Camera, photo library and photos

The app uses the camera or photo library only when you choose to take or select a photo. Barcode, nutrition-label and meal photos are analysed on your device. Original photos are not uploaded or saved with your log. After you confirm, only the food and nutrition information you choose is saved. You can change camera and photo-library access in iPhone Settings.

Export, deletion and external links

In Settings → Your data, you can export a JSON backup, import a backup or delete local data. You choose where to save an export. If you save it to a cloud-drive provider or share it, that provider or recipient controls its later handling. A backup may contain sensitive health and food records; keep it secure. Export before changing devices or deleting the app. We cannot recover local data that was not backed up.

Data-source links in the app open third-party websites only when you choose to follow them. Those websites have their own privacy practices.

Contact

If you email support, we receive your email address and the content you provide, and use them to handle your request. Do not email backup files, medical documents or passwords. Contact: studionorthbyte@gmail.com.

If cloud sync or another data-collecting feature is introduced later, we will update this policy and the App Store privacy details before release.
