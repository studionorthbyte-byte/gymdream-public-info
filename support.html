gymdream 支援

免費公開版本機儲存訓練、飲食和體重記錄，並不提供雲端備份。換機、清除資料或刪除 App 前，請到「設定 → 你的資料 → 匯出備份」，再把 JSON 檔妥善存放。需要還原時可在同一頁揀「匯入備份」。

相片營養只係粗略估算：份量、油和醬料等要由你確認。App 不提供疾病飲食建議或自動飲食目標；如有特殊健康情況，請向你的醫生查詢。

遇到問題可電郵 studionorthbyte@gmail.com，請寫明 iPhone 型號、iOS 版本、App 頁面與出錯步驟。不要附上備份檔、私人相片、密碼或醫療文件。

gymdream Support

The free public release stores workouts, food and weight logs locally; it does not provide cloud backup. Before changing phones, clearing data or deleting the app, use Settings → Your data → Export backup and store the JSON file safely. Use Import backup on the same page to restore it.

Photo-based nutrition is only a rough estimate. Confirm portions, oil, sauces and other details yourself. The app does not provide condition-specific diet advice or automatic diet targets. Consult your doctor about special health conditions.

For help, email studionorthbyte@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version, the app screen and steps to reproduce. Do not attach backups, private photos, passwords or medical documents.
